1. Introduction

prism-ocr-solver is an on-device floating assistive study and quiz analysis tool. We are deeply committed to user privacy, data minimization, and user control. This policy describes how our application collects, uses, and protects your information.

2. Data Handling & Security

A. Screen Capture & OCR Processing

When you trigger a scan via the floating Dynamic Island or Bubble HUD, the app captures a temporary bitmap of your current screen strictly in volatile random-access memory (RAM). Google ML Kit performs optical character recognition locally on your device. Bitmaps are promptly deleted once analysis completes and are never saved to disk or persistent storage.

B. Artificial Intelligence Inference

  • Method 1 (Server Sol - Firebase AI Logic): Extracted question text is encrypted and sent to Firebase AI Logic powered by Gemini to generate the solution.
  • Method 2 (OCR Text Extractor - Background Gemini): Employs on-device OCR and background Gemini processing.
  • Method 3 (User Local API Keys): If you configure your own API keys (NVIDIA NIM, OpenRouter, OpenAI, Google AI), requests are made directly from your device to the respective provider. Your keys are stored strictly in private, sandboxed device storage and are never uploaded to any intermediate server.
